Pseudo-classes that can be evaluated during optimization should not be toggled
through the usePseudos parameter.

See: 

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 50 50">
  <style>
    path:not([fill=blue]) {
      stroke: purple;
    }
  </style>
  <path fill="red" d="M5 5H10"/>
</svg>
